title=Immersive Looted Buildings
description=Shop loot disappears little by little over time, the way it would after the end of the world. Markets and pharmacies lose the most, then survival stores, and small shops lose the least. The loss is fastest in the first week and slows down as survivors die off. Places overrun by zombies, like hospitals, prisons, police and the army, were never raided, so they keep extra loot.
description=
description=[b]How it works[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] Every shop loses loot gradually. No shop is simply raided or intact.
description=[*] Each priority has a chance. That share of its shops loses the full amount and ends with only the minimum loot. The other shops lose less, each one a different amount that never changes.
description=[*] Nothing disappears in front of you. The loss is applied when a shop is filled or loaded, before you see it. While you are close to a shop or looting it, nothing is removed. The loss shows up the next time the shop loads, after you left.
description=[*] Entering a shop or opening one of its containers pauses its loss for 14 days. After that it restarts slowly from where it stopped.
description=[*] A shop you use as a base never loses loot. It counts as a base once you stored 3 items there or used it on 3 different days. Items you put in a shop container are never removed, base or not.
description=[*] Buildings the game itself already looted, with the vanilla looted buildings option, are left alone.
description=[*] Houses and any room that is not in the lists are never touched.
description=[/list]

description=[b]Sandbox options[/b]
description=Everything is in the sandbox settings. You can set the start, peak and end day of the loss, the slowdown, and an exact day and percent curve if you want one. Each priority has its own chance, loot at the peak and minimum loot. You also set how much less the other shops lose, the overrun extra loot, and the room list of each priority, so map mods can add their own rooms. There is the protect radius, the recent visit pause, the base thresholds, useful items taken first, loot respawn and decision logging.
description=
description=[b]Mid-save[/b]
description=You can add or remove the mod in a save that is already running. Shops that were already filled get today's loot the first time they load with the mod. Items you stored before adding the mod have no mark, so they can be lost like the shop's own loot. Removing the mod leaves the save intact.
description=
description=Rooms the game fills from the map and not from loot tables are not covered. That includes clothing stores, bookstores, army surplus, pawn shops and a few others. Zombies, vehicles and items lying on the floor are not covered either.
