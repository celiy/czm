title=Immersive Looted Buildings
description=Shops are raided over time, the way they would be after the end. Markets and pharmacies go first, survival stores next and small shops last. Looting is fastest in the first week and slows down as survivors die off. Places overrun by zombies (hospitals, prisons, police and army) were never raided and keep extra loot.
description=
description=[b]How it works[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] Each shop building is either raided or still intact, with a chance that grows from day 0 to day 360. A raided shop keeps only part of its loot; an intact one keeps all of it.
description=[*] The decision is per building and never changes back: a shop raided on day 30 is still raided on day 100.
description=[*] Shops you have only seen are raided when you open them later, before you see their contents (no items vanish in front of you).
description=[*] Buildings you use are protected: a shop you only looted and left can be raided 30 days later; a shop you live in (items stored or several days there) only after 60 days without you. Items you put in a shop container are never removed.
description=[*] Buildings the game itself already looted (the vanilla looted buildings option) are left alone; by default the mod counts them, so the total matches the table below.
description=[*] Houses and any room not in the lists are never touched.
description=[/list]
