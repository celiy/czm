title=Immersive Looted Buildings
description=Shops get raided over time, the way they would after the end of the world. Markets and pharmacies go first, then survival stores, and small shops last. Looting is fastest in the first week and slows down as the survivors die off. Places overrun by zombies, like hospitals, prisons, police and the army, were never raided, so they keep extra loot.
description=
description=[b]How it works[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] Every shop building is either raided or still intact. The chance of being raided grows from day 0 to day 360. A raided shop keeps only part of its loot, and an intact one keeps all of it.
description=[*] The decision is made once per building and never goes back. A shop raided on day 30 is still raided on day 100.
description=[*] A shop you have only seen gets its decision the first time you open it later on. You never see its contents vanish in front of you.
description=[*] Buildings you use are protected. A shop you only looted and left can be raided 30 days later. A shop you actually live in, with items stored or several days of visits, is only raided after 60 days without you. Items you put in a shop container are never removed.
description=[*] The game's own looted buildings option is respected. Buildings the game already looted are left alone. By default the mod counts them, so the total still matches the table below.
description=[*] Houses and any room that is not in the lists are never touched.
description=[/list]

description=[b]Sandbox options[/b]
description=Everything is in the sandbox settings. You can turn the mod on or off, set the start, peak and end day of the looting, and choose the share done at the peak for each tier. There is also the slowdown, and an exact day and percent curve if you want one. Each tier has its own chance and loot cut. Overrun places get their extra loot, and every tier has a room list, so map mods can add their own rooms. The rest covers cut variation, a fully stripped chance, useful items taken first, counting the game's looted buildings, applying the mod to containers already filled, the protection windows and base thresholds, cutting abandoned buildings, applying it on loot respawn, and decision logging.
description=
description=[b]Mid-save[/b]
description=You can add or remove the mod in a save that is already running. Containers already filled are handled the first time you open them, and buildings not loaded yet get today's status. Removing the mod leaves the save intact.
description=
description=Rooms the game fills from the map and not from loot tables are not covered. That includes clothing stores, bookstores, army surplus, pawn shops and a few others. Zombies, vehicles and items lying on the floor are not covered either.
