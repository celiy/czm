title=Perishable Foods
description=[b]IMPORTANT:[/b] if you add this mod to a save that is already going, the food and drinks you own start fresh the first time the mod sees them. Loot in places you have not visited yet is aged from the day you add the mod so nothing rots retroactively.
description=
description=In vanilla, most food in Project Zomboid never spoils. Milk, juice, soft drinks, biscuits, chocolate and jam keep forever. Here they do go bad, each with a believable shelf life. Fridges and freezers slow it down, sealed alcohol keeps, and loot in places nobody has visited is already old when you find it.
description=
description=[h2]Add-on for Vanilla Foods Expanded[/h2]
description=This item has a second, optional mod in the mod list called Perishable Foods: Vanilla Foods Expanded. Turn it on only if you also use Vanilla Foods Expanded. Without that mod it will not start.
description=
description=Vanilla Foods Expanded compatibility:
description=[list]
description=[*] Spices, dried herbs, seasoning mixes and bouillon cubes last 2 years.
description=[*] Candy, chocolate, snacks, nuts, dried fruit, rice, pasta, ramen, flour, baking mixes, jarred sauces, jams, pickles and olives, tea bags, creamers and soft drinks last 1 year.
description=[*] Cereal, granola, croutons and jelly or pudding cups last 6 months.
description=[*] Fudge lasts 3 months, packaged cakes and strudels 1 month, homemade sauces 1 month.
description=[*] Boxed juices and iced teas last 4 months, milks 2 months and juices 1 month.
description=[*] The shelf life multiplier of the main mod applies to the add-on too. Fridges and freezers work the same way.
description=[/list]
