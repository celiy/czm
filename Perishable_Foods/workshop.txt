title=Perishable Foods
description=[b]IMPORTANT:[/b] when you add this mod to a save that is already in progress, food and drinks you already own start fresh the first time the mod sees them. Loot in places you have not visited yet is aged from the day you add the mod, so nothing rots retroactively.
description=
description=In vanilla, much of the food in Project Zomboid never spoils: milk, juice, soft drinks, biscuits, chocolate, jams, sauces... Here they do, each with a believable shelf life. Fridges and freezers slow it down, sealed alcohol keeps, and loot in places nobody has visited is already old.
