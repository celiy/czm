description=[*] Fully configurable in sandbox options: start day, days until fully degraded, curve shape, minimum liters for containers and vehicles, pumps on/off.
description=[*] Safe to add or remove mid-save. Adding starts the clock from that day; removing leaves the save intact.
description=[/list]
