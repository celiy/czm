title=Gasoline Degradation
description=[b]IMPORTANT:[/b] if you add this mod to a save that is already running, set "Start day" to the day the save is on right now. If you don't, the decay is counted from day 0 and hits all the gas you own the moment you come back.
description=
description=Gasoline loses quality over time in vehicles, gas pumps and any container that holds it. So it pays to stock up early, and late game fuel is hard to find. Gas goes bad.
description=
description=[b]Features[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*] Works everywhere: vehicle tanks, gas pumps, gas cans and any other container with gasoline in it. It also covers other mods that use the vanilla fluid and vehicle systems.
description=[*] Everything is set in the sandbox options. You can change the start day, how many days until the gas is fully degraded, the curve shape, the minimum liters for containers and vehicles, and turn the pumps on or off.
description=[*] Safe to add or remove in the middle of a save. Adding it starts the clock from that day, and removing it leaves the save as it is.
description=[/list]
description=
description=By default a full 10 L gas can drops to about 2 L after 3 months. Everything is down to the minimum after 6 months.
